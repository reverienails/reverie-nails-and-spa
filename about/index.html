About our Saginaw nail salon

Reverie Nails and Spa has been open in Saginaw since 2020. We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin'. The salon is locally owned by Joseph.

At Reverie, we blend exceptional quality and technical skill with everyday care and glamorous touches. Our technicians are encouraged to build real connections — getting to know you as you get to know them.

We envision Reverie as the community’s favorite “watering hole” — a warm, welcoming space where old friends reconnect and new ones are made. Our open layout and signature circular nail table are designed to spark conversation and connection.

Whether you’re here for a luxurious pedicure or a quick set of nails, we hope you leave feeling relaxed, refreshed, and a little more connected. The studio was designed to feel like a second home.

How we keep the salon clean

Metal implements are sanitized in an autoclave. Single-use items, like files and buffers, are used once and thrown away.
