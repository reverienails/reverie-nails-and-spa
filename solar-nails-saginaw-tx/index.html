Solar nails in Saginaw, TX

Solar nails at Reverie are the pink-and-white sets, the color sets, and the ombre, done in our Saginaw salon. We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin'. A pink and white full set is $55. A pink and white fill is $50. A pink fill is $35.

A solar color set is $45, and a same-color fill is $40. Solar ombre is $60, and a solar ombre fill is $55. Those are the menu prices. Extra length, a shape change, and nail art start at $5 if you want them.

Walk in while we are open, or book a time if you want a specific technician. Call (682) 224-5688. People come from Saginaw, and from Fort Worth, Eagle Mountain, Lake Worth, and Haslet. Sit down with a cocktail, wine, or water. Drinks are complimentary.

A solar set takes around 45 minutes to an hour, the same window as dip. Art and length can add time. Acrylics and builder gel are separate services if you are comparing sets.
