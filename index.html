Nail Services

Gel Manicure
Basic professional care for your natural nails and surrounding skin. Includes shaping, cuticle work, and optional treatment upgrades. Can be paired with Gel, Dip, Acrylics, Builder Gels, or Gel-X.
Acrylics
Overlays on natural nails or extensions created using nail tips. Made with colored powder (Solar) and can be finished with gel polish. Extremely strong and excel at longer-than-normal lengths. Fully fillable and ideal for French tips and detailed designs.
Dipping Powder
Single-use sets created from dipping powder. Works on both natural nails and artificial extensions. Capable of delivering everything from solid colors and French tips to beautifully designed sets.
Builder Gel & Gel-X
A thick, self-leveling gel (Builder Gel) or pre-made soft gel tips (Gel-X) cured under UV/LED light. Offers flexible strength with a more natural feel than acrylic. Ideal for longer nails, especially milky, sheer, or dimensional color techniques that benefit from depth and clarity. Fully fillable.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer student or military discounts?
Yes — we offer special pricing every day for active-duty military, veterans, and their immediate families, plus teachers and students. Just mention it when you book.
What nail types do you offer?
Natural manicures, dipping powder, Acrylics, Solar Nails, French/ombre nails, Designs, Builder Gel, Gel-X and extra pampering if needed
Are drinks really complimentary?
Yes. Every guest gets a drink during their service. Cocktails, wine, rosé, and water are on the house.
Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. You can also book ahead online if you want a specific technician or a locked-in time so you can get in quicker.
What should I expect during my first visit?
We’ll welcome you, go over your service, and make sure you’re comfortable. Our goal is for you to leave feeling relaxed and happy with your results.
Do you take walk-ins?
Yes. Come by Ste 101 during open hours — no appointment needed. Booking ahead online is optional if you want a particular tech or a set time.
