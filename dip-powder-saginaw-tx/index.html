Common questions

Do you take walk-ins for dip?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. Booking ahead helps if you want a specific tech or a set time.

How long does a dip take?

Around 45 minutes to an hour. Art, length, or a French tip can add time. If yours will run longer, we will say so when you sit down.

How much is dip powder?

A color set is $40. Adding tips is $5. The Classic Dip Manicure is $52. The Aloe Vera Dip Manicure is $60. The Reverie Organic Dip Manicure is $68. A dip ombre is $55. Prices are starting points and can change with length, shape, and nail art.

Do you warranty a dip?

We warranty the nail for 2 weeks.

What is the difference between a color set and a dip manicure?

A color set is the dip color, at $40. A dip manicure is the longer manicure around the dip. Classic is $52, Aloe Vera is $60, and Reverie Organic is $68.

Can I get dip on my natural nails?

Yes. Dip works on natural nails and on extensions. Adding tips is $5.

Can I add French tips, length, or nail art?

Yes. Adding tips is $5. French is $5 to $15, depending on the curve and the color. Length and designs start at $5. A new shape is $5.

Where are you and is there parking?

We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, Saginaw, TX 76179. Free parking right outside: park in any open spot in our lot.

Do you offer discounts?

Monday through Thursday before 3 PM, all services are 15% off. Can't be combined with other offers. Military, teachers, and students get special pricing every day. Mention it when you book.
