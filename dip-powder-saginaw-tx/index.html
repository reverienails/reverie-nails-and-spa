Dip powder in Saginaw, TX

Dip powder at Reverie is done in the chair, at our Saginaw salon. We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin'. You pick a color, we do the set, and you have a drink while you sit.

A color set is $40. Adding tips is $5. If you want the longer manicure around the dip, the Classic Dip Manicure is $52, the Aloe Vera Dip Manicure is $60, and the Reverie Organic Dip Manicure is $68. A dip ombre is $55. Those are starting prices. Length, shape, and nail art can change the total, and those add-ons start at $5 on the full menu.

Walk in while we are open, or book ahead for a specific technician or a set time. Call (682) 224-5688 if you would rather talk to us. Guests come from Saginaw, Fort Worth, Eagle Mountain, Lake Worth, and Haslet.

Cocktails, wine, rosé, and water are complimentary with your service. The menu does not promise one wear-time for dip. Ask us about the set you want, and we will tell you what to expect.
