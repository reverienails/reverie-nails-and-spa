Common questions

Do you take walk-ins for pedicures?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. Booking ahead helps if you want a specific tech or a set time.

How long does a pedicure take?

From 30 minutes for the Classic to 70+ minutes for Diamond VIP and Red Glow.

Can I get gel polish on my toes?

Yes. Regular polish is complimentary with your pedicure. Adding gel to any pedicure is $20. Gel on toes by itself is $25.

How often should I get a pedicure?

Every 3–4 weeks keeps feet and polish in good shape.
