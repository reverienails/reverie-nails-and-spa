Gel nails here are color on your natural nails. A gel manicure follows the same care as the matching classic manicure, then finishes with gel polish. Gel Polish Only (hands) is $25 when you want the color without the full manicure.

Common questions

How long does a gel manicure last?

We tell guests a gel manicure usually holds for 2 to 3 weeks when they look after it.

What is the difference between a gel manicure, builder gel and Gel-X?

A gel manicure is gel polish color on your natural nails. Classic Gel is $38. Builder gel adds strength on the natural nail. A full set is $55 and a fill is $50. Gel-X is soft gel extensions for length. A full set is $70, and a fill with builder gel is $50.

How much is a gel manicure?

Classic Gel is $38. Aloe Vera Gel is $48. Reverie Organic Gel is $58. Gel Polish Only (hands) is $25. Gel removal is $10, and it is free with a new gel service the same visit.

Can I add gel to my pedicure?

Yes. Add gel to any pedicure for $20, or gel on toes on its own for $25.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. Booking ahead helps if you want a specific tech or a set time.

How long does a gel manicure take?

A gel manicure takes about 45 minutes. A classic manicure takes around 20 minutes. Art, length, or a French tip can add time.

Do you charge to remove gel?

Gel removal is $10. It is free when you are getting a new gel service the same visit. That includes a gel manicure, builder gel, Gel-X, or gel on a pedicure.

Can I get just gel color without a full manicure?

Yes. Gel Polish Only (hands) is $25. That includes a nail cut, file and shape, and gel color. It does not include cuticle work.

Do you offer discounts?

Monday through Thursday before 3 PM, all services are 15% off. Can't be combined with other offers. Military, teachers, and students get special pricing every day. Mention it when you book.

Where are you and is there parking?

We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, Saginaw, TX 76179. Free parking right outside: park in any open spot in our lot.
