Gel-X Nails in Saginaw, TX

Gel-X at Reverie is a full set, done in the chair. Soft gel tips, shaped and finished here. It is not a kit you take home. A full set is $70. A fill with builder gel is $50.

A thick, self-leveling gel (Builder Gel) or pre-made soft gel tips (Gel-X) cured under UV/LED light. Offers flexible strength with a more natural feel than acrylic. Ideal for longer nails, especially milky, sheer, or dimensional color techniques that benefit from depth and clarity. Fully fillable.

Gel-X is one of three kinds of gel nails we do in Saginaw. The others are a gel manicure for color on your natural nails and builder gel for strength.

We are at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin'. People come from Saginaw, and from North Fort Worth, Eagle Mountain, Blue Mound, Lake Worth, and Haslet.

Sit down and have a cocktail, a glass of wine, or any of our other complimentary drinks while the technician works on designing your nails. Art and length can add time to the service, so sit back, sip, and enjoy.

Walk-ins are welcome whenever we are open. If you want a certain technician, or a time you can plan around, book ahead.
