Acrylic nails in Saginaw, TX

Acrylic sets at Reverie are done in house, at our nail salon in Saginaw. The address is 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin'. A full set with regular polish is $40. A fill with polish is $35. A full set with gel polish is $55, and a fill with gel is $50.

Come by as a walk-in while we are open. Book ahead when you want a specific technician or a locked-in time. You can also call (682) 224-5688. Neighbors come from Saginaw, Fort Worth, Eagle Mountain, Lake Worth, and Haslet.

Complimentary drinks are part of sitting down. Cocktails, wine, rosé, and water are the ones guests ask for most. French tips run $5 to $15 depending on the curve and the color. Length, shape, and designs start at $5.

Solar pink and white and builder gel are their own services, with their own pages, if that is the set you want. An acrylic set takes around 45 minutes to an hour, the same window as dip. Art and length can add time.
