Nail salon FAQ in Saginaw, TX

Answers for Reverie Nails and Spa at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101. If your question is not here, call (682) 224-5688.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. You do not need an appointment. Book ahead at https://book.reverienailstx.com/book if you want a specific technician or a set time. You can also call (682) 224-5688.

Where do I park?

Free parking is right outside. Park in any open spot in our lot. We are in Ste 101, in the same plaza as Dunkin', at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Saginaw, TX 76179.

How much do services cost?

Prices are on the menu and can vary with length, shape, and nail art. A classic manicure starts at $20, a classic pedicure at $30, a gel manicure at $38, dip color at $40, an acrylic full set with polish at $40, builder gel at $55, solar pink and white at $55, and Gel-X at $70. Kids 8 and under have their own menu, starting at $15 for a manicure.

How long do services take?

A manicure takes around 20 minutes. Dip, acrylics, builder gel, and solar take around 45 minutes to an hour. Gel-X takes around an hour. Art and length can add time. Pedicures run from 30 minutes for the Classic to 70+ minutes for Diamond VIP and Red Glow.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are for a dollar amount you choose.

How can I pay?

Guests can pay with cash, card, or tap.

Do you have spa packages?

We do not sell a separate spa package. The longer pedicures are the spa side: massage, hot stones, paraffin, and the luxe menu, including Rosé All Day, Diamond VIP, and Red Glow. Prices and times are on the pedicure page.

Do you have services for kids?

Yes. Kids 8 and under can get a manicure for $15, a pedicure for $25, polish on hands or toes for $10, gel on hands for $18, or gel on toes for $20.

How do you sanitize your tools?

Metal implements are sanitized in an autoclave. Single-use items, like files and buffers, are used once and thrown away.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give as much notice as you can if you cannot make an appointment. Repeated no-shows may affect future booking. We do not charge a cancellation fee on this website.

Where are you, and when are you open?

Reverie Nails and Spa is at 4613 W Bailey Boswell Rd, Ste 101, Saginaw, TX 76179. Monday through Friday 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and Sunday 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Are drinks included?

Yes. Drinks are complimentary with your service. Cocktails, wine, rosé, and water are what most guests want. The full drink list is on the menu.

Do you offer military, teacher, or student pricing?

Yes. Military, teachers, and students get special pricing every day. Mention it when you book. Monday through Thursday before 3 PM, all services are 15% off. That offer cannot be combined with other offers.
